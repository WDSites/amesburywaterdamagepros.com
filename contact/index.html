Call dispatch

Have these ready when you call: your address and the nearest main road, what is wet and where the water came from, how long the area has been wet and whether anyone has been near electrical equipment. Photos on your phone help your insurer later, so take them before you move anything.

If water is near an outlet, a panel or a gas appliance, call the utility or 911 about the electrical or gas risk first, then call us. Never promise yourself a wet wall will dry on its own.
