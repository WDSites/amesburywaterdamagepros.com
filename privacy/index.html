Privacy Policy

How we handle calls and website data.

This policy explains how Amesbury Water Damage Pros, 77 Elm Street, Amesbury, MA 01913, handles information when you call us or visit this site. Last updated October 29, 2026.

Calls and recordings

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your name, phone number, address and a description of the damage so we can route the job. We use that information to dispatch a crew and to follow up on your request.

Website data

This site does not have forms or accounts. Our hosting provider and standard web tools may log technical data such as pages visited, browser type and the approximate region of a visit. We use that data to keep the site working and to understand which pages are used.

Sharing

We share call details with the restoration professionals who handle your job, and with service providers who help run the site and phone line. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete information you gave us by calling (978) 252-8637 or emailing contact@amesburywaterdamagepros.com. Tell the dispatcher at the start of a call if you do not want it recorded, and we will note the request.

Changes

We may update this policy, and the date at the top shows the latest version.
